What’s New
v1.1 · recent camera and filter support
Show release notes
This release imports the latest System Compare and Exposure Explorer catalog work into the spectral-response teaching view, with weak-proxy items called out where the source data is less complete.

New cameras

  • QHY247C, marked as weak QE-proxy support
  • QHY183C
  • QHY MiniCam8M and MiniCam8C
  • ZWO ASI678MM and ASI678MC
  • ZWO ASI294MM Pro Bin 1 and Bin 2 readout-mode entries

New filter support

  • Baader IR-Cut Moon & Skyglow Neodymium
  • SVBONY SV260 Multi-Bandpass, marked as estimated / provisional spectral support
  • SVBONY SV220 Ha/OIII 7nm
  • SVBONY SV220 SII/OIII 7nm
  • SVBONY SV220 Ha/OIII + SII/OIII combo workflow

Model notes

  • Spectral Explorer now mirrors the new camera and filter choices added to System Compare and Exposure Explorer.
  • ASI294MM Pro Bin 1 is treated as a distinct readout mode rather than ordinary software binning.
  • Weak or estimated curves remain labeled in the UI so they are not mistaken for lab-grade spectral measurements.
